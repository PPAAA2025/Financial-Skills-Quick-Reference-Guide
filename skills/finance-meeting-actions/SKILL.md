# Finance Meeting Actions

## Workflow

1. Confirm the source, meeting date, participants as provided, intended audience, and whether the transcript may contain confidential data.
2. Separate reported facts, estimates, discussion/opinions, confirmed decisions, and unresolved questions.
3. Extract action items only when a task is stated or clearly agreed. Capture owner and due date only when explicit; otherwise mark them unassigned or not specified.
4. Preserve financial amounts, units, periods, and qualifiers. Flag conflicting figures and ask for confirmation rather than reconciling by guess.
5. Keep sensitive personnel, customer, banking, and transaction details out of broad-distribution minutes unless necessary and authorized.

## Deliverables

- Concise minutes with date and source scope.
- Confirmed decision log, with exact status.
- Action tracker: action, owner, due date, dependency, and status.
- Open questions and ambiguous statements.

Do not send minutes, assign tasks in an external system, or represent a discussion as a decision unless the user explicitly requests that action.
