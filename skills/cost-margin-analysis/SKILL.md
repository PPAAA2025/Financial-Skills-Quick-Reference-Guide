# Cost and Margin Analysis

## Workflow

1. Confirm the unit of analysis, reporting period, currency, revenue recognition basis, and the approved direct-cost and allocation policies.
2. Inspect source revenue and cost records. Check duplicates, missing keys, negative quantities, timing, intercompany entries, and whether taxes are included.
3. Map costs to the selected product/project/customer only using supplied identifiers or approved allocation drivers. Do not choose a new allocation method silently.
4. Calculate revenue, direct cost, allocated cost, gross contribution, and margin using explicit formulas. Keep unallocated or disputed costs separate.
5. Compare periods or segments on a like-for-like basis. Decompose changes into volume, price, mix, and cost only when the data supports that analysis.
6. Flag negative/declining margins and data-quality exceptions. Label conclusions as calculated fact, interpretation, or hypothesis.

## Deliverables

- Segment-level margin table and reconciliation to source totals.
- Material movement analysis and traceable source rows.
- Unallocated costs, assumptions, and policy questions.

Do not describe a segment as profitable or loss-making when material costs are unallocated. Management must approve policy decisions and actions.
