# Finance Policy and SOP

## Workflow

1. Confirm the process, intended users, effective policy versions, and desired output (SOP, checklist, flow, or form field guide).
2. Extract requirements from supplied policies and forms. Record document name, section, and version for each substantive rule.
3. Organize the process by trigger, role, input/evidence, action, approval, timing, output, and exception path.
4. Compare forms and policies for conflicting thresholds, roles, required documents, or approval paths. Do not resolve a conflict by choosing one rule silently.
5. Mark missing ownership, undefined terms, and proposed clarifications separately from existing policy.
6. Write concise instructions in plain language, preserving thresholds and mandatory approvals exactly as supplied.

## Deliverables

- Draft SOP or role-based checklist with source references.
- Required-input and evidence list.
- Exceptions, contradictions, and approval questions.
- Clear draft status; do not represent it as an approved policy.

Only an authorized policy owner can approve or publish revised procedures.
