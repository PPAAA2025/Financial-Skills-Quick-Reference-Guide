# Budget Variance Analysis

## Workflow

1. Confirm reporting period, comparison baseline, currency, entity, accounting basis, and whether favorable/unfavorable signs differ by line item.
2. Map budget and actual account/department codes. Keep unmapped records visible and obtain approval for any mapping assumptions.
3. Reconcile imported totals to source reports. Identify missing periods, duplicate rows, accrual timing differences, and changes in classification.
4. Calculate amount and percentage variance. Avoid misleading percentages when the baseline is zero or immaterial; show the amount and label the percentage not meaningful.
5. Rank variances using a materiality threshold provided by the user, or present a clearly labeled proposed threshold for approval.
6. Explain a variance only when supported by supplied evidence. Tag commentary as evidenced, management explanation, or hypothesis; do not infer cause or assign responsibility from correlation alone.
7. Produce follow-up questions for unresolved drivers and distinguish one-off from recurring items only when evidence supports it.

## Deliverables

- Summary by entity, department, and account.
- Material variance detail with formula and source references.
- Commentary status and questions for budget owners.
- Mapping decisions, exceptions, and tie-out results.

Preserve source values and formulas. Do not overwrite the original budget or actuals.
